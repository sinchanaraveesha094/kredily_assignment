## Risk assessment
Data-consistency defects (BUG-01, 02, 06) matter most in an HRMS: they affect approvals and attendance/payroll trust. UI defects are cosmetic.

## Coverage and gaps
Covered: login, home, approvals, attendance, directory, profile. **Not covered:** leave application, regularization submit, payroll, face/selfie clock-in, offline sync, other roles (employee/manager).
